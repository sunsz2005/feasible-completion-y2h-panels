For seeds 20261113 to 20261132, permute the sorted 8,342-edge list with NumPy default_rng(seed). Put the first 6,673 edges into training and the remaining 1,669 into evaluation. Use the same split and search seed for A and B. Do not load evaluation partitions during optimization.

Use panel size 200, balanced exclusive roles of 100 each, coverage parameter q=0.8, threshold 0.98 times the training reference coverage, and the unchanged A/B construction and common joint solver. Calibrate T0 once per training split by reference coverage selection, fixed-panel role partition, reference coverage calculation, and completion-set construction. Retain all completed paths. The timing tolerance is max(0.02 seconds, 5% of the allowance).
